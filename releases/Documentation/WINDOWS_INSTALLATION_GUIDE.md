# BI Antivirus Enterprise — Windows Laptop Installation Guide
**Black Intel Cyber Defense Platform — Official User Guide**

## Welcome to BI Antivirus!
BI Antivirus Enterprise is engineered to protect your Windows laptop from viruses, ransomware, malicious downloads, and phishing websites without slowing down your system.

---

## 1. Quick Installation (Recommended)

1. **Download the Installer:**
   - Locate `BI_Antivirus_Enterprise_v1.0.0_Setup.exe` in your `releases\Windows\Installer\` folder.
2. **Launch Setup:**
   - Double-click `BI_Antivirus_Enterprise_v1.0.0_Setup.exe`.
   - *Note:* The installer does not require administrative elevation to install for your user account.
3. **Follow the Steps:**
   - Choose your preferred shortcuts (Desktop shortcut is created automatically).
   - Click **Install**.
4. **First Launch & Setup Wizard:**
   - Check **Launch BI Antivirus Enterprise** and click **Finish**.
   - The **Welcome Setup Wizard** will appear to configure your protection in under 60 seconds.

---

## 2. Using the First-Run Setup Wizard

The wizard will guide you through four simple steps:
- **Step 1 (Welcome):** Overview of your new cybersecurity platform.
- **Step 2 (Device Health):** Confirms that your computer has sufficient memory and storage, and enables seamless coexistence with Windows Defender.
- **Step 3 (Protection Profile):**
  - **Smart Shield (Recommended):** Balanced protection with silent background scanning and zero false alarms.
  - **Fortress Shield:** Maximum enterprise protection with strict heuristic scanning and automatic USB drive inspection.
- **Step 4 (Complete):** Confirms your green **PROTECTED** status and runs a quick baseline scan.

---

## 3. How to Open the App

You can open BI Antivirus at any time via:
- The **BI Antivirus Enterprise** shortcut on your Desktop.
- The **Start Menu** under **BI Antivirus Enterprise**.
- Double-clicking the shield icon in your system tray.

---

## 4. How to Update

BI Antivirus includes an easy, automated update system:
1. Open the application and click **Settings** (or **About**).
2. Click **🔄 Check Software Updates**.
3. If new virus definitions or engine enhancements are available, they will be verified with cryptographic signatures and installed safely without interrupting your work.

---

## 5. How to Uninstall

If you ever need to remove BI Antivirus:
1. Open **Windows Settings > Apps > Installed apps**.
2. Find **BI Antivirus Enterprise** and click **Uninstall**.
3. The uninstaller will ask:
   *"Do you want to retain your encrypted Quarantine Vault and threat history?"*
   - Choose **Yes** if you plan to reinstall later and want your locked files kept safe.
   - Choose **No** to perform a complete wipe.
